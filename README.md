# Àkànjí Oníṣòwò

> *Àkànjí Oníṣòwò* (ah-KHAN-jee oh-nee-SHAW-woh) — Yoruba for *"Àkànjí the trader."*

## The origin

Àkànjí is a real man. A living identity. A seasoned trader — from the physical
markets of West Africa to global financial floors, and now deep in the Web3
space. Decades of experience. One constant truth: he sees the market before
the market moves.

The name carries weight. In Yoruba, *Àkànjí* speaks to one who has earned
wisdom through lived practice — not theory. *Oníṣòwò* means *trader*, but
more than that: it means one who *knows* the market, the way a sailor knows
the sea.

This agent is the AI embodiment of that spirit. He doesn't talk about trading
— *he trades*. Watches, decides, sizes, enters, manages exits, reports. All
without prompting. All without permission. All without emotion.

When Àkànjí greets you, he greets you in Yoruba:
- *Káàrọ̀* — good morning (before noon, WAT)
- *Káàsán* — good afternoon
- *Káàlẹ́* — good evening
- *Káàlẹ́ òru* — good night

## What this is

Àkànjí Oníṣòwò is an **autonomous trading agent** for the **OKX.AI Trading
Hackathon Season 1** ($40,000 USDT prize pool). He trades on **X Layer**
(eip155:196) using **Onchain OS**, with discipline borrowed from the physical
markets and the humility of one who knows that no trader wins every battle.

He does not chase. He does not YOLO. He waits for confluence, sizes with
respect for the account, and exits with a plan. Every position has a stop.
Every day has a limit. Every loss has a ceiling.

Built fresh. No bloated framework. No LLM in the hot path. No magic. Just
clear rules, hard limits, and a heartbeat that doesn't stop.
